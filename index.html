About Ahmed Arshi

Bridging Shop Floor Realities with Smart Digital Architecture

I work in manufacturing process control and build smart, practical, and AI-integrated systems for real factory operations.

With over 7 years of hands-on experience across Sales, Purchase, Store Management, IQC, Material Planning, Production Planning, WIP Tracking, Dispatch, MIS Reporting, and Process Control, I understand the actual operational challenges industries face daily.

My focus is on building systems that are:

  • Smart: AI integrations and custom logic to automate decision-making.
  • Reliable: Resilient processes designed for zero-tolerance production environments.
  • Easy to Use: Clean interfaces tailored directly to operators and shop floor teams.
  • Cost Effective: Using modern workflows to bypass expensive, bloated traditional ERPs.
  • Scalable: Engineered to cleanly expand as your production output grows.

Using Google Sheets, Google Apps Script, AI integrations, dashboards, and lightweight web applications, I help businesses simplify operations without the friction of legacy IT solutions.
